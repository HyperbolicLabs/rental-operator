# rental-operator governance

rental-operator lets teams rent Hyperbolic GPUs from their own Kubernetes clusters, safely and declaratively. This document explains how the project is run.

Hyperbolic sponsors the project and employs its current maintainers. Contributions from anyone are reviewed on their merits, and contributors from outside Hyperbolic can become maintainers through the process below.

## Values

- Discussion and decisions happen in public, in issues, pull requests and [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/HyperbolicLabs/rental-operator/discussions), so they can be found later.
- Everyone can give feedback and send contributions, and they are considered on their merits.
- Different perspectives make the project better, which takes a welcoming and respectful environment.
- Responsibility is earned by taking part, and the path to becoming a maintainer is written down.

## Maintainers

Maintainers have write access to the repository and can merge pull requests, their own or others'. The current maintainers are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Together they form the Maintainer Council, which governs the project and manages its resources.

A maintainer is someone who cares about the project and helps it grow. That means collaborating with the team, getting the right people to review code and docs, contributing high-quality work, and following through on issues.

### Becoming a maintainer

To become a maintainer, a contributor shows:

- commitment to the project, by taking part in discussions and reviews for three months or more, reviewing five or more non-trivial pull requests, and getting five or more non-trivial pull requests merged
- the ability to write quality code or documentation
- the ability to collaborate with the team
- an understanding of how the project works, its code base and its style

An existing maintainer proposes a new maintainer in a public GitHub Discussion. A simple majority of the maintainers approves. Nominations are evaluated without regard to employer, and the council values maintainers from more than one organization.

### Stepping back and removal

A maintainer may step back at any time. A maintainer may also be removed after twelve months of little or no activity, for not fulfilling their responsibilities, or for violating the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Removal takes a two-thirds vote of the other maintainers.

A maintainer who steps back or is removed for inactivity can become an emeritus maintainer, listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Emeritus maintainers are recognized for their contributions and may be consulted, but they do not vote or merge. A simple majority of the maintainers can restore an emeritus maintainer who meets the current requirements.

## Meetings

The project has no regular public meeting yet. Maintainers meet in private only to handle security reports and code of conduct reports.

## Code of conduct

Reports of [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) violations are handled privately by the maintainers. If a maintainer is involved in a report, that maintainer takes no part in handling it.

## Security response

The maintainers handle security reports, following the [security policy](SECURITY.md). They may appoint a security response team of at least two people instead, and they review that assignment at least once a year.

## Decisions and voting

Most decisions are made by [lazy consensus](https://community.apache.org/committers/lazyConsensus.html) in issues, pull requests and Discussions. Any maintainer may call for a vote. Votes take place in a public Discussion, or privately for security and conduct matters. A vote passes with a simple majority of all maintainers, unless this document says otherwise. A two-thirds vote means at least two-thirds of all maintainers.

## Evolving this governance

The Maintainer Council model suits a small, focused project. As the project grows, the maintainers will revisit this document, for example when decisions stall because the group is large, when contributors need intermediate roles such as reviewer, or when maintainers from more organizations join and voting should be balanced across them.

## Changing this document

Changes to this document need a two-thirds vote of the maintainers.
